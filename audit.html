What an Audit Is

A diagnosis, not a demo

An AI audit is a structured look at how your work actually moves today. It ends with a short list of changes worth making, ranked by payoff.

We build nothing during the audit. Building too early is how businesses end up with a clever tool aimed at the wrong problem.

Sometimes the answer is an off-the-shelf tool. We name it, price it, and give you the first step.
Sometimes it is a Claude skill built around a workflow that only your business has.
Sometimes the process is broken. Then we say to fix it first, because automating a bad process makes it fail faster.
Sometimes the answer is to leave it alone. The part that already works stays as it is.

What We Ask You

The questions behind the numbers

Every recommendation in the report rests on your own numbers. These are the questions that produce them.

The workflow

Where the time goes today

  1. Walk me through how this works today, start to finish.
  2. Where does it slow down or break most often?
  3. If you could fix one thing in your business with a wave of a wand, what would it be?
  4. What have you already tried, and what happened?

The cost of the problem

What it costs you to leave it alone

  1. How many hours a week does your team spend on it?
  2. Who does that work, and what do they cost per hour?
  3. How often do things go wrong, and what does one mistake cost to fix?
  4. What is this stopping you from doing that would grow the business?

We also take a quick inventory of the work in five areas: sales, operations, finance, marketing, and the information you rely on to make decisions. We look at the ten tools you use most and any AI you already pay for. And we ask what you would do with ten extra hours a week, because the plan points at that answer.

How We Rank

What goes first, and why

We rank by two things. How often the problem happens, and how much friction it causes each time. A task that is both frequent and painful goes to the top. Then we weigh the effort of the fix against what it gives back.

Each finding then gets one of four answers.

Use an off-the-shelf tool

The problem is common and someone has already solved it well. Cheapest and fastest.

Use Claude Cowork

The problem takes judgment, and a general-purpose assistant can carry it with the right instructions.

Build a Claude skill

The workflow is specific to your business, so no product fits it. A skill captures how you do it.

Fix the process first

Steps that never needed to exist get cut before anything is automated.

What We Don't Do

The limits, stated up front

We don't build during the audit

You pay for the diagnosis and the plan. Any build is a separate decision you make with the report in hand.

We don't pitch you more than you need

If three small changes solve it, the report says three small changes. A short report is a fine outcome.

We don't need your logins or customer data

The audit runs on conversation and a screen share you control. In regulated industries we stay on back-office work and never touch client data.

We don't lock you in

No long-term contracts. The report is yours, and every first step in it can be done without us.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to be technical?

No. The audit is a conversation about how your business runs. You describe the work and show it on a screen share. We handle the technical side and write the report in plain language.

Do you need access to my systems or customer data?

No. The audit runs on conversation and a screen share of your own workflow. We do not need logins, and you choose what to show. For regulated industries we stay on back-office work and never touch client data.

What if the audit finds nothing worth automating?

Then the report says so. Sometimes the best advice is to protect the part that already works, fix a broken process first, or simplify what surrounds it. The report is a diagnosis, not a sales pitch.

Do I have to hire you after the audit?

No. The report is yours and every first step in it can be done without us. If you want help doing the work, the AI Concierge is there, with no long-term contract.

Does the audit build anything?

No. The audit diagnoses and prescribes. Building starts only after you have the report and choose to go further.
