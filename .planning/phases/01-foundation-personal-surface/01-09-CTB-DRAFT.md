## Body (~280 words)

### What it is

Cross The Bridge is the brand for the consulting work — AI implementation, agent integration, privacy and self-hosted-infrastructure setup, and the strategy work that ties them together. It serves people and small teams who want to opt out of legacy systems without going off-grid: Bitcoin instead of banks, self-hosted compute instead of surveillance Cloud, AI you own instead of AI that owns you.

### Why it exists

The world most of us grew up in is no longer the world we live in. The institutions that mediated money, communication, and daily life have rebuilt themselves around extracting behavioral data and steering attention. Most of the tools sold as "convenience" are now the toll booths. The rules have changed; the playbooks haven't caught up.

Cross The Bridge exists to help people make the crossing deliberately — Money, Data, Infrastructure — while it's still possible to choose. Sovereignty as a service: the work of getting from where the defaults landed you to a stack you actually own.

### Where the deeper work lives

The product side — Petros, the foundation under all of this; AYLIP, the long-arc framework — lives at the Cross The Bridge site itself. This page is the introduction. The depth, the consulting offers, the FAQ, and the engagement paths all live on the brand site.
