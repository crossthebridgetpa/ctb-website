
Identical formatter signatures in both layouts. Both render dates from `entry.data.published` and `entry.data.updated` which are coerced to UTC `Date` objects by `z.coerce.date()` in `content.config.ts`. Without `timeZone: 'UTC'` in the options object, the formatter applies the host's local timezone to the UTC Date — producing different visible strings on hosts in different timezones.
