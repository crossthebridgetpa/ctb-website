## Files Created/Modified

## Decisions Made

- **Strict regex (no digits):** locked in 02.1-CONTEXT.md before planning. Verified against the 19-tag corpus — every existing tag matches. No digit-bearing tags today, so the strict form costs nothing and gives the strongest typo protection.
- **Refined at schema, not at consumer:** `src/lib/tags.ts` is consumer-only (aggregates already-validated tags into topic pages). Per the plan's `<interfaces>` note, refining at `content.config.ts` is the canonical chokepoint. `src/lib/tags.ts` was not modified.
- **Did NOT refine the `related` field:** slugs are filename-derived. A future essay could legitimately be `bitcoin-2026.md` and its slug would contain a digit. WR-07 scope is tags-only per the lock; expanding to `related` would create a false positive when that day comes.
- **Single atomic commit instead of two task-commits:** the plan's Task 2 explicitly says "create the atomic commit" and the success criteria say "single atomic commit references WR-07." Splitting into two commits (one for the edit, one for verification) would conflict with that direction.
